Import fixtures from playfiveaside

Copy the league fixtures page and paste it below — every remaining fixture gets created in one go.
  1. Open the league fixtures page ↗ (opens in a new tab)
  2. Select everything on that page — ⌘A (Mac) or Ctrl+A (Windows)
  3. Copy it — ⌘C / Ctrl+C
  4. Come back here and paste into the box below — ⌘V / Ctrl+V
  5. Press Read fixtures, then confirm the list
Don't worry about the mess — scores, goalscorers and adverts are stripped out automatically. Nothing is saved until you confirm.
